About CareSync

Making healthcare easier to reach.

CareSync is a healthcare access and navigation platform connecting patients, facilities, and administrators across Nigeria.

Project overview

CareSync connects patients, healthcare facilities and administrators through facility discovery, intelligent matching, referral tracking and emergency healthcare information.

The problem

People struggle to find facilities offering the services they need, to trust the information they find, to navigate referrals between providers, and to use digital health tools on limited connectivity.

Mission

Give everyone clear, reliable, and accessible information to reach appropriate healthcare — regardless of location, language, or connectivity.

Vision

A healthcare system where finding and following care is straightforward for every patient and provider.

Core objectives

  • Improve discovery of facilities and services
  • Make information status transparent and verifiable
  • Simplify referral coordination between providers
  • Surface emergency contacts quickly

Accessibility goals

  • Works on small phones and slow networks
  • English and Yoruba, with room to grow
  • Keyboard and screen-reader friendly
  • No colour-only status signals

Product values

Honesty: information status is always displayed. Privacy: we minimise collection of sensitive health data. Safety: CareSync never replaces professional medical care or emergency services.

What CareSync is not

Not a hospital management system, diagnosis tool, ambulance dispatch service, or live bed-availability tracker. It is an access and navigation platform.
