How it works

From question to care, step by step.

Five parts of the CareSync experience, explained plainly.

1

Finding care

Search by facility name, location or service, then filter by facility type and accessibility features.

2

Comparing facilities

Use Smart Matching to rank facilities on your needs. Each result explains which factors raised or lowered its score.

3

Understanding facility information

Look for the status badge: Verified, Pending or Unverified, plus when details were last updated.

4

Referral guidance

Track a referral through Pending, Accepted, In progress, Completed or Declined.

5

Emergency information

See nearby facility contacts and directions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

Does CareSync show hospital bed availability?

No. CareSync never shows availability unless verified data is provided by a facility.

Can CareSync diagnose my condition?

No. It helps you find facilities. It does not give medical advice.

Does the match score mean a facility is medically best for me?

No. It is a transparent demonstration score based on service fit, distance, accessibility, verification and information freshness.

Is the data on this site real?

No. All facilities and referrals shown in this demo are synthetic.

CareSync does not replace local emergency services or professional medical care.
